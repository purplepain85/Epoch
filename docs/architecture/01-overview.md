## What this is

A platform for running local sports content markets. One hub. Many markets.
Each market serves one team, in one city, to one audience.

The hub produces. The operator steers. The audience talks.

## The thesis

Aggregation is the floor. Interaction is the ceiling.

Anyone can aggregate news. RSS feeds, subreddits, beat writers, podcasts —
the raw material is public and abundant. Aggregation is a service, but it is
not a business. Feeds get scrolled past.

What sells is participation. Fans come to argue, agree, rant, and be heard.
The article is the prompt. The conversation is the product. Every
interaction is a reason to come back.

## The pieces

- **Hub.** One system, in one place, serving every market.
- **Pipeline.** Auto and manual modes. Sources news, spins it, formats it.
- **Spin Filter.** Per-market voice. Tone, angle, emphasis.
- **Outputs.** Reddit, Facebook, X, TikTok. Same content, platform-native.
- **Media.** Podcasts, videos, existing fan content. Surfaced, not produced.
- **Interactions.** Comments, re-shares, rants. Pre-cast, post-cast.
- **Event Log.** Every step recorded. Tamper-evident. Yields the year-end cut.
- **Revenue.** Affiliate, ads, podcast shares. Per-market settlement.
- **Operator.** The local face. Brings the audience. Steers the market.

## What makes it defensible

Not the code. Not the templates. Not the domain.

The concept — pipeline plus rules plus attribution plus revenue structure
plus operator model, assembled in one place. Anyone could copy it after it
exists. Until then, the one who built it is the only one who has it.

## What it isn't

Not a franchise. Not a subscription. Not a SaaS.

The operator keeps the recurring revenue. The hub takes a year-end cut
based on performance. Nobody pays upfront.

## Build order

Bottom-up:

1. Event Log (the memory)
2. Hub (the runtime)
3. Pipeline (the producer)
4. Outputs (the shape)
5. Interactions (the point)
6. Media (the volume)
7. Spin Filter (the voice)
8. Revenue (the settlement)
9. Operator (the person)
10. Onboarding (the entry)

Everything above this file is a note on one of those ten.

## Status of decisions

Locked in by conversation, not yet tested:

- One hub, many markets
- Manual paste first, auto search second
- Interaction is the product
- Pre-cast and post-cast threads both live
- Podcasts integrated, not produced
- Year-end cut, no upfront fee
- Buyout prices on every clause
- Colors and logos travel together, full team name appears only for credit

## Gridiron Central

You already run a fan community. Maybe it's a Facebook group. Maybe it's a
subreddit. Maybe it's an X account that half of your city follows. You've
built it over years. You post every day, answer every question, keep the
peace, and celebrate every win.

You do all of this for free.

Gridiron Central gives you the tools to run that community as a real
business. You bring the audience. We bring everything else.

## What "everything else" means

- A pipeline that produces content for you — news rehashed in your voice,
  ready for Reddit, Facebook, X, and TikTok without you writing a word
- A comment and rant layer that turns readers into participants
- Podcast integrations that pull in the shows your fans already listen to
- An affiliate and ad layer that pays you real money
- A dashboard showing what's working, what isn't, and where to push
- A year-end cut structure that rewards you for growing the market

You bring the audience. We bring the machine.

## Who this is for

This is not for someone who wants a side project.

This is for the fan who is already doing the work — the moderator who
keeps a 40,000-person group civil, the account runner who lives and dies
with every fourth-quarter drive, the person in your city who knows every
player's name and every reporter who covers the team.

It is for the fan who is already all-in and has been all-in for years.
Dedication, moxie, and the willingness to keep showing up every Sunday
and every Wednesday and every off-season.

If that is you, this turns what you're already doing into something
that pays.

## The season

You compete alongside your team. When your team goes on a run, your
market goes on a run. When the fanbase is loud, your market is loud. When
your team reaches January, your market reaches its biggest month of the
year.

You don't own the team. You don't play for the team. But you carry the
conversation, week to week, and the fanbase follows you the same way it
follows the season.

## The money

Affiliate revenue, ad revenue, podcast revenue. You keep the recurring.
Gridiron Central takes a year-end cut based on how your market performed.
Nobody pays upfront. If your market doesn't earn, we don't earn. The
incentive is the same for both of us.

## The scale

32 markets. One per team. First come, first served.

When they're gone, they're gone. Positions that close stay closed until
an existing operator exits.

## The ask

If this sounds like you — if you're already doing the work and you're
ready to get paid for it — read `02-the-market.md` next.

If it doesn't sound like you, no hard feelings. This isn't for everyone.

It's for the fan who thinks they can run it.

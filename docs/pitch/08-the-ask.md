## What we're asking

One position per team. Thirty-two total.

If you're reading this because you were invited, you're a candidate for
one specific market — your team, your city. If you take it, no one else
takes it. If you pass, it goes to the next candidate.

## What you commit to

- **You bring the audience.** Your existing group, page, subreddit, or
  server becomes the foundation of the market. If you don't have an
  audience yet, this isn't for you.
- **You show up.** Weekly during the season. Monthly in the off-season.
  You don't have to produce content — the pipeline does that. You have to
  be present for the community.
- **You run the community.** Respond to comments. Moderate threads. Keep
  the peace. Flag anything that looks wrong.
- **You find the podcasts.** The team-friendly shows in your city. You
  know them. Add them to the market. Negotiate terms within the standard.
- **You make the local calls.** Which stories to push. Which to skip.
  Which need a different angle. The pipeline offers defaults. You steer.
- **You follow the rules.** The brand rules protect the whole network.
  If you violate them, the position can be revoked.

## What we commit to

- **The pipeline.** Runs continuously. Produces content in your voice,
  formatted for every platform, ready to publish.
- **The tools.** Dashboard, event log, affiliate redirector, podcast
  integrations. All hosted. All maintained.
- **The rules engine.** Applies the brand rules automatically. You don't
  have to remember them.
- **The license.** You use the platform and the network under our name.
  Small monthly fee. Small routing fee on affiliate traffic.
- **The support.** When something breaks, we fix it. When you have
  questions, we answer.
- **The network.** As other markets come online, your market benefits
  from the national layer.

## What you keep

- Your accounts
- Your audience
- Your podcast relationships
- Your sponsored content deals
- Every dollar you earn outside the routing fee
- Your reputation in your city

If you ever walk away, those walk with you. What stays with the hub is
the pipeline and the event log. Everything else is yours.

## What you don't pay

- No upfront fee
- No monthly minimum
- No cut of your ad revenue
- No cut of your podcast revenue
- No cut of your sponsored content
- No cut of the affiliate revenue you earn directly

The only money that changes hands:

- A small monthly license fee for the platform
- A small routing fee on affiliate traffic that runs through the hub

Everything else, you keep.

## The timeline

If you accept, here's what happens:

- **Week 1.** Onboarding call. Market configured. Colors, voice, output
  channels set up. First podcast relationships scoped.
- **Week 2.** First content runs through the pipeline. You review it
  before it publishes. You tune the spin filter.
- **Week 3.** Market goes live. You announce it to your existing
  audience. The first comment threads open.
- **Week 4.** First affiliate revenue lands. You see the dashboard in
  action. You start making local calls.
- **Month 2.** First podcast integrates. First sponsored content if the
  timing is right. First real numbers on the board.
- **Month 3.** The market is running. You know what works. You know what
  doesn't. You adjust.

## What happens if you decline

The position goes to the next candidate on the list.

There's no penalty for declining. If the timing is wrong, or you don't
want the responsibility, that's fine. The position stays open until
someone takes it.

You can be considered again for a future opening if one arises.

## What we need from you to move forward

- **A yes or no** on taking the position
- **Your team** and your existing audience (the group, the page, the
  accounts you'd bring in)
- **A short call** to walk through the deal details
- **A signature** on the operator agreement
- **A first week** where we set everything up together

That's it. From yes to live market is about three weeks.

## The one question to answer

If you're reading this and you already know this is for you, reply yes.

If you're reading this and you're not sure, ask your questions.

If you're reading this and it's not for you, no hard feelings.

We're looking for 32 people. If you're one of them, you already know.

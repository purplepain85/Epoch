## Who we're looking for

Not "someone interested in football." Not "someone who likes the team."
Not "someone who can write."

We are looking for the person who is **already doing the work**.

If they already run a Facebook group, a subreddit, an X account, a
Discord server, a podcast, or a blog about one team, they are the
candidate. If they're active every week, if they post during games, if
they moderate their own community, if they've done it for at least one
full season, they qualify.

The test is simple: **have they already been doing this for free?**

If yes, they're a candidate. If no, they're not.

## What we're screening for

- **Dedication.** They post in the off-season, not just during games.
- **Audience.** They have at least a few thousand engaged followers.
- **Moxie.** They have opinions. They defend them. They take the heat.
- **Discipline.** They moderate. They don't let trolls eat the community.
- **Local knowledge.** They know the beat writers, the podcasts, the
  team's history, the city's culture.
- **Reliability.** They've shown up every week for a full season.

If they have all six, they're a strong candidate. Missing one or two is
negotiable. Missing three means they're not ready.

## Where to find them

### By team

Every team has fan communities. The same pattern plays out in every city:

- **Facebook groups** — usually the largest, often moderated by 1-3 people
- **Subreddits** — r/{teamname} with active moderators
- **X accounts** — team-focused accounts with 5K-100K followers
- **Discord servers** — especially common in the last two years
- **Team blogs** — SB Nation sites, FanSided sites, independent blogs
- **Podcasts** — fan podcasts, usually run by 1-3 people

The recruitment targets are the **individuals** who run those. Not the
communities themselves. Reach out to the person, not the page.

### By channel

- **Reddit** — DM the moderators of the team subreddit
- **X** — DM accounts that post actively about one team
- **Facebook** — message the admins of the largest team group
- **Discord** — join the server and ask who runs it
- **Podcast contact emails** — usually listed in the show notes

### The international angle

The NFL has fan groups in every major market now. Germany, Mexico, the
UK, Brazil, Japan, Australia. In those markets there are fewer candidate
operators, but the ones who exist are extremely committed — they run
fan clubs, watch parties, translation accounts, and newsletters.

International operators can be the strongest candidates. They've had to
work harder to follow the team, so their dedication is proven by
definition.

## The outreach

### First contact — direct and short

    Hi — I'm building a platform for team-focused content and I'm
    looking for one operator per team. You run [group/page/subreddit]
    for [team] and it looks like you've been doing it for [years/season].
    I'd like to talk to you about turning that into paid work with tools
    that run it for you. Can I send you more info?

Short. Direct. Mentions their team. Mentions their existing work. Doesn't
pitch yet. Doesn't ask for a meeting. Just gets a yes/no on more info.

### Second contact — the one-pager

Send them `01-the-idea.md`. That's the hook. If they read that and reply,
they're interested. If they don't reply, they weren't a strong enough
candidate to chase.

### Third contact — the call

If they're still interested, set up a 30-minute call. Walk them through
`02-the-market.md`, `03-the-operator-deal.md`, and `04-the-revenue-model.md`.
Answer questions. Don't sell. Just explain.

### Fourth contact — the offer

If they're in, onboard them. Show them the dashboard. Set up their market.
Help them integrate their first podcasts. Watch the first week together.

## The pitch in one paragraph

    You already run [team] content for free. We built a system that
    produces it for you, formats it for Reddit, Facebook, X, and TikTok,
    tracks every affiliate conversion, and logs everything so you can
    see what works. You keep your audience, your accounts, and every
    dollar you earn. We take a small license fee and a routing fee on
    affiliate traffic we process. No upfront, no cut of your business.
    We're only doing this for 32 markets. Yours is one of them. Want
    in?

## What to send with the pitch

- `01-the-idea.md` — the hook
- A screenshot or short video of the pipeline running
- A sample market page — if one is live, this is the strongest proof
- The pitch paragraph above
- A clear next step

## The first operator

The first operator is the hardest. Everything depends on them. If they
succeed, they become the case study. If they fail, the model is
unproven.

Pick the first operator carefully. Choose someone:

- With a large, engaged audience
- Who has been running their community for years
- Who is known and respected in their team's fanbase
- Who is willing to work with a new system

For the first market, the operator is effectively a co-founder. Give
them the deal that reflects that. Take less from the first market than
you would from the rest. The first market's success is worth more than
the money.

## What happens after the first market works

Once one market works, the rest are easier. You have proof. You have
numbers. You have a case study. The pitch becomes:

    One market is live. Here's what it looks like. Here's what the
    operator earns. Here's what the pipeline produces. We have 31
    positions left.

That's a much easier sell than a theoretical pitch. The second operator
comes faster than the first. The tenth comes faster than the second. By
the time you're recruiting for the twentieth market, candidates are
reaching out to you.

## Realistic timeline

- **Month 1:** Build the pipeline. Prove it works on one team's content.
- **Month 2:** Recruit the first operator. Launch market one.
- **Months 3–6:** Run market one. Document everything. Refine the pitch.
- **Months 6–12:** Recruit operators 2–10. Launch first ten markets.
- **Year 2:** Recruit operators 11–20.
- **Year 3:** Recruit operators 21–32. Complete the network.

Three years to full coverage. Not fast. But every year builds on the
last.
